## Monthly Health Check Workflow

Perform a comprehensive association health check every month.

<Steps>

1. ### Analyze Overall Health

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Give me a complete health assessment of our association"
   ```

   This runs `analyze_membership_health` and provides:
   - Total member count and engagement distribution
   - Risk factor identification
   - Statistical analysis (mean, median, percentiles)

2. ### Review Churn Risks

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Show me members at risk of churning in the next 90 days"
   ```

   This runs `get_churn_risk_report` and identifies:
   - Members with upcoming renewals
   - Risk scores and intervention recommendations
   - Predicted churn rate and revenue impact

3. ### Check Financial Health

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Analyze our payment health and aging"
   ```

   This runs `analyze_payment_health` and shows:
   - Collection rates and outstanding balances
   - Aging analysis (30/60/90+ days)
   - Payment trends

4. ### Compare to Previous Period

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Generate a membership snapshot compared to last month"
   ```

   This runs `generate_membership_snapshot` with comparison:
   - Growth metrics and trends
   - Breakdown by chapter, type, and status
   - Key performance indicators

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  **Pro Tip:** Schedule this workflow for the same day each month (e.g., first Monday) to track trends consistently.
</Aside>

## Retention Campaign Workflow

Launch a targeted retention campaign for at-risk members.

<Steps>

1. ### Identify At-Risk Segments

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Find all members who are at-risk across multiple factors"
   ```

   This runs `find_all_at_risk_members` to identify:
   - Payment delinquency
   - Low engagement
   - Short tenure members
   - Poor communication response

2. ### Analyze Engagement Patterns

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "What patterns do you see in our most engaged vs least engaged members?"
   ```

   This runs `identify_member_patterns` to discover:
   - What drives engagement
   - Common risk factors
   - Successful retention strategies

3. ### Segment Members

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Segment all members by risk level"
   ```

   This runs `segment_all_members` with strategy "risk":
   - Critical risk members
   - High risk members
   - Medium risk members
   - Low risk members

4. ### Create Targeted Lists

   Ask Claude:
   ```
   "Export critical risk members with their contact info and risk factors to CSV"
   ```

   Get data ready for:
   - Email campaigns
   - Personal outreach lists
   - CRM import

5. ### Track Progress

   After 30 days, ask Claude:
   ```
   "Show me engagement score changes for members who were at critical risk last month"
   ```

</Steps>

## Best Practices

### Effective Queries

**Be Specific**

❌ **Too Vague:** "Show me members"
✅ **Better:** "Show me active members from downtown chapter with outstanding balances over $500"

**Use Natural Language**

✅ "Find members who joined this year but haven't attended any events"
✅ "Who's at risk of not renewing in the next 60 days?"
✅ "Give me our top 10 most engaged members"

**Ask Follow-Up Questions**

Claude maintains context, so you can ask:
1. "Show me at-risk members"
2. "Now filter that to just critical risk"
3. "Export those to CSV with contact info"

### Data Management

**Association Filtering**

Always set `ramco-association-id` in your configuration to:
- ✅ Prevent cross-association data access
- ✅ Improve query performance
- ✅ Simplify all queries

**Query Optimization**

- Use bulk tools (analyze_membership_health) instead of individual queries
- Export large datasets to CSV for offline analysis
- Cache frequently accessed reports

**Rate Limiting**

- Limit: 100 requests per minute per IP
- Use bulk analytics tools (they count as single requests)
- Avoid loops of individual record queries

### Interpretation Tips

**Engagement Scores**

- **75-100**: Highly engaged (celebrate these members!)
- **50-74**: Moderately engaged (nurture and grow)
- **25-49**: At-risk (intervention needed)
- **0-24**: Inactive (urgent action required)

**Risk Levels**

- **CRITICAL**: Immediate personal intervention (call, meeting)
- **HIGH**: Targeted outreach within 7 days
- **MEDIUM**: Include in re-engagement campaigns
- **LOW**: Standard retention activities

**Churn Prediction**

- Focus on members within 30 days of renewal
- Prioritize high-value/long-tenure members
- Use recommended interventions as guidelines

### Intervention Strategies

**For At-Risk Members**

1. **Personal Touch**: Phone call from membership chair or director
2. **Event Invitation**: 3-5 specific events matching their interests
3. **Committee Recruitment**: Match skills to committee needs
4. **Value Reminder**: Highlight benefits they're not using
5. **Feedback Request**: "How can we serve you better?"

**For Payment Issues**

1. **30 Days**: Friendly reminder email
2. **60 Days**: Personal call offering payment plan
3. **90+ Days**: Escalate to collections process
4. **Always**: Make payment easy (online options, flexible plans)

**For New Members (First 90 Days)**

1. **Week 1**: Welcome email with getting started guide
2. **Week 2**: Personal call from membership team
3. **Month 1**: Invite to new member orientation event
4. **Month 2**: Committee fair invitation
5. **Month 3**: Check-in survey and engagement scoring

## Advanced Techniques

### Comparative Analysis

Compare time periods:
```
"Show me membership growth this quarter vs last quarter"
"Compare event attendance this year vs last year"
```

### Predictive Insights

Ask for patterns:
```
"What patterns predict successful retention?"
"What factors lead to high engagement?"
"What's the common profile of members who renew vs those who don't?"
```

### Custom Segmentation

Create specific segments:
```
"Find members who:
- Joined in last 2 years
- Haven't attended events in 6 months
- Are on at least one committee
- Have no outstanding balance"
```

### Batch Operations

Process multiple related queries:
```
"Give me a complete executive summary including:
1. Membership health analysis
2. Churn risk report for next 90 days
3. Payment health with aging
4. Growth comparison to last quarter"
```

## Troubleshooting

### No Results Returned

**Check:**
- Is `ramco-association-id` set correctly?
- Does the data exist in RAMCO for the query?
- Are you using the correct entity type names?

**Try:**
```
"Test my RAMCO connection"
"Tell me about my association"
```

### Unexpected Results

**Verify:**
- Association ID matches your intended association
- Date ranges are correct (remember: today's date is in results)
- Filters are using correct RAMCO field names

**Debug:**
```
"Show me the filter being applied to this query"
"Return this query as JSON so I can see the exact data"
```

### Rate Limiting

**If you hit rate limits:**
- Wait 1 minute for reset
- Use bulk tools instead of loops
- Combine multiple questions into one query

**Example:**
❌ **Bad:** Ask 50 separate questions about individual members
✅ **Good:** "Analyze engagement for all members" (one query)

## Tips for Success

### Regular Monitoring

- **Weekly**: Quick health check, review critical alerts
- **Monthly**: Full health assessment, retention review, financial analysis
- **Quarterly**: Pattern analysis, strategy adjustment, trend comparison
- **Annually**: Comprehensive review, goal setting, forecasting

### Documentation

Keep notes on:
- Intervention strategies that work
- Engagement patterns unique to your association
- Seasonal trends (renewals, events, etc.)
- Member segments and their characteristics

### Continuous Improvement

Track metrics over time:
- Retention rate improvements
- Engagement score trends
- Collection rate changes
- Response rates to interventions
